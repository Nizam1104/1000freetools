## Guidelines Reminder

### Writing Style (writing-humanizer.md)
- Avoid AI patterns: no "testament to", "pivotal moment", "evolving landscape"
- No promotional language: no "nestled", "vibrant", "groundbreaking"
- Use simple constructions (is/are/has) instead of "serves as", "stands as"
- No em dash overuse, no emojis in content
- No generic positive conclusions
- Add personality and voice - vary sentence rhythm
- Be specific about feelings and details

### SEO Guidelines (seo-guidelines.md)
- SEO Title Tag: 55-60 chars with primary keyword
- Meta Description: 145-155 chars, conversational, soft CTA
- H1: Match search intent, natural keyword
- Intro: 60-80 words, lead with user's problem
- How It Works: 3 short steps as plain sentences
- Features/Benefits: 4-5 items with bold labels
- FAQ: 5 questions matching real search queries
- Related Tools: 3 natural anchor text links
- Add tables and charts (Recharts) where appropriate
- Write genuinely useful content like omnicalculator.com and calculators.net
